Anti-Homograph Extension

Warns about domain characters that resemble English letters.

Check extension status in Safari Settings.

Enable website access

Enable AHE in Safari Settings > Extensions, then allow access to the websites you want checked. Open the toolbar popup to check the current domain.

Warnings do not block connections or certify that a site is safe. A page may load before the warning appears.

Privacy policy · Support

Checks run on your device. No browsing history, analytics, or personal data is saved or transmitted.
